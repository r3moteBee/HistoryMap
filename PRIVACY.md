# History Map — Privacy Policy

Applies to History Map for Safari and for Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers.

**History Map does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.**

## What the extension stores
To draw your browsing map, History Map records the following on your device:

- The addresses (URLs) and titles of web pages you visit in normal browsing windows.
- When each page was visited, and which page you came from.
- Notes you write and display settings you choose (colors, shapes, layout, filters).

URL fragments and common tracking parameters are removed before anything is stored.

## Where it is stored
Everything is kept in the extension's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) on your device. It never leaves your device: History Map makes no network requests, has no server, and includes no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Private browsing windows (Safari Private Browsing, Chrome Incognito, and equivalents) are not recorded unless you explicitly allow the extension in them in your browser's settings.

## Your control
- **Export data** saves a copy of your history, notes and styles as a file that only you control.
- **Clear…** permanently deletes all recorded history and notes.
- Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.

## Website access
The extension asks for access to all websites only so the browser lets it see page addresses and titles as you browse. It does not read, change or collect page content.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the extension.
